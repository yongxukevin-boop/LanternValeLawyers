Business legal service plans

Legal support for growing teams.

Plan terms effective 1 July 2026. Available to businesses with 50 to 500 employees, inclusive, in Georgia.

Plans and pricing

Business Essentials

US$110 per employee per month when billed annually, or US$130 per employee per month when billed monthly. A minimum of 50 employees is charged. Setup is US$500 once.

Includes contract reviews and business consultations. Client portal single sign-on is not included.

Business Enterprise

Includes the Essentials services, plus client portal single sign-on and a named relationship manager. Enterprise pricing is quoted separately.

Neither plan includes litigation representation or tax-return preparation.

Consultation options

Business consultations can take place by video or in person. Urgent business support is available Monday–Friday, 8 am–6 pm Eastern time; it excludes public holidays.
